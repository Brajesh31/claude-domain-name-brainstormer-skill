# Domain Candidate Evaluation Rubric

Score each candidate from 1 (weak) to 5 (strong).

| Criterion | What to assess |
|---|---|
| Memorability | Can the intended audience recall it after hearing it once? |
| Pronunciation | Is the likely pronunciation obvious? |
| Spelling | Can someone type it correctly without explanation? |
| Distinctiveness | Does it stand apart from category competitors? |
| Relevance | Does it support the positioning without over-describing? |
| Flexibility | Can the brand expand beyond its initial product? |
| Cultural safety | Are there unwanted meanings in target languages or markets? |
| Searchability | Is it likely to be confused with common terms or established brands? |

Use weighted scoring only after agreeing on priorities. A high score does not establish trademark clearance or domain availability.

## Hard-stop checks
Reject or flag names that:
- imitate an established brand or imply affiliation;
- depend on deliberate misspelling to obtain a domain;
- create an offensive or misleading meaning in a target market;
- are difficult to say, hear, or type;
- create an avoidable legal or reputational risk.
